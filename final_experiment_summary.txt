IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
------------------------
Historical 95th-percentile deviation values are reference values only.
They are NOT validated crop-stress thresholds.

The current dataset contains no independent crop-stress labels,
field measurements, LAI measurements, biomass measurements,
or verified crop-history labels.

Therefore the deviation signal is interpreted as
observation-model disagreement, not validated crop stress.

The crop value in the exported dataset should not be treated
as verified ground truth crop identity.

CURRENT SCIENTIFIC CLAIM
------------------------
The study demonstrates a sequential Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2/weather
latent-state digital twin with EnKF-based data assimilation on a
2024 temporal holdout.

Assimilation reduces the free-running twin's S2 observation-space
RMSE by approximately 20.5%.

The assimilating twin does not outperform the seasonal baseline
on overall 2024 RMSE.

Stress detection is not claimed as validated in the current
real-world dataset.
